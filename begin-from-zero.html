Part 1: Grammar Rules

Affirmative:(Afirmativa)>

• I/You/We/They + verb (work, play, study)

• He/She/It + verb + s (works, plays, studies)


Negative: (Negativa)

• I/You/We/They + don't + verb

• He/She/It + doesn't + verb


Questions: (Perguntas)

• Do + I/you/we/they + verb?

• Does + he/she/it + verb?
